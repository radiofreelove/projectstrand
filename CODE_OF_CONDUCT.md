# Code of Conduct

The Strand Project exists for trans, nonbinary, and intersex people and the people who help them. Every space connected to this project (issues, pull requests, comments, and any chat or meeting) must be safe for them.

## Expected

- Treat people with respect and assume good faith.
- Use the names and pronouns people give.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Protect privacy: never share anyone's personal or case details.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats, or insults of any kind.
- Misgendering on purpose, deadnaming, or debating anyone's identity.
- Outing anyone, or sharing private information without consent.
- Content that demeans people for gender identity, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, disability, race, ethnicity, religion, age, body, or immigration status.
- Spam or bad-faith "corrections" meant to mislead readers.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers by opening an issue that says only that you need to report a conduct problem and how to reach you. Do not post details publicly. Reports are handled privately. You do not need to share personal details to report.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn, or block anyone who breaks this code, at their discretion and in proportion to the harm. Blocking can be immediate for threats, outing, or doxxing.
