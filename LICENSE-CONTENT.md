# Content license (proposed)

Status: proposed, pending the maintainer's decision.

The written content of The Strand Project (the words on the site, in `templates/pages/`, and in `docs/`) is proposed for release under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** license (CC BY 4.0).

In plain words, anyone may share and adapt the content, including for commercial use, as long as they:

- give credit to The Strand Project,
- link to the license, and
- say whether they made changes.

Full legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Why this license: legal aid groups, court self-help centers, schools, and people in other states should be able to reuse and adapt this guide without asking.

Quoted laws, court forms, and government text belong to their original publishers and are not covered by this license.
