# Reusing The Strand Project

You can copy, share, and change this project, even in work you sell. Please credit us. Legal aid groups, court self-help centers, schools, and people in other states are welcome to adapt it.

## Which license covers what

| What | License | Full text |
|---|---|---|
| **The writing:** the words on the site (in `templates/pages/` and `data/`), plus `docs/` and the other Markdown files | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) | [LICENSE](../LICENSE) |
| **The code:** `build.py`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `static/` (CSS, JavaScript, and the mark), `.github/`, and the HTML and template code in `templates/` | MIT License | [LICENSE-CODE](../LICENSE-CODE) |

Page templates hold both. The words in them are writing (CC BY 4.0). The HTML and template code around the words are code (MIT).

Plain-language summary of CC BY 4.0 from Creative Commons: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

## How to credit us

Creative Commons suggests a credit with four parts: title, author, source, and license. A link to each is best.

Using a page as it is:

> "Oregon: change your name and legal sex" by The Strand Project (https://projectstrand.pages.dev/oregon), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Using a page you changed:

> Adapted from "Oregon: change your name and legal sex" by The Strand Project (https://projectstrand.pages.dev/oregon), CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). We shortened it and added our county's court address.

The license asks you to say whether you made changes. A short note like the last sentence above is enough.

For code: keep the copyright line and the MIT permission notice in your copy, as the MIT License asks.

## What these licenses do not cover

- **Laws, court forms, and government pages** we link to or quote. They belong to the people who publish them.
- **The optional font,** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. It is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The build downloads it and saves its license text next to the font files.
- **Our name and mark.** You may use them to credit us or to point people to us. Please do not use them to make a copy look like it is ours, or like we approve it. CC BY 4.0 says the same about the writing: it does not license trademarks (section 2(b)(2)), and it gives no permission to suggest we endorse your use (section 2(a)(6)).

## A request, not a rule

The law changes, and an old copy can send someone to the wrong form. If you republish a page, please keep the date it was last checked and link back to our current page. The licenses do not require this.
