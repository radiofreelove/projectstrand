# Roadmap

## Before 1.0 (launch)

- Legal review of each state's pages by a lawyer licensed in that state.
- Paid testing with trans, nonbinary, disabled, and neurodivergent people, including screen reader users. Fix what they find.
- Pin font hashes; make the axe-core job blocking after reviewing its first results.
- Close the open questions in the audit log (Utah license and Vital Records fees, Oregon footnotes after confidential cases, State Department page text).
- Choose the licenses and settle the hosting address.

## Next

- **Spanish**, with legal review, then other languages common in each state.
- **Email intake for corrections** so readers do not need a GitHub account.
- **Alerts from scheduled checks**: the weekly freshness and link reports run already; route their results to the maintainer.
- **Printable packets**: one-page PDFs per state for court self-help centers and clinics.
- **"What to bring" lists** for each office visit.
- **Change alerts**: a public changelog page and an optional feed when a rule changes.

## Later

- More states, starting with neighbors and high-need states, one research memo at a time.
- Partnerships with legal aid groups to review and share content.
- A custom domain.
