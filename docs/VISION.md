# Vision

## The one sentence

Changing your name or gender marker should not take a lawyer, a law degree, or luck.

## The problem

Getting ID that matches who you are is a legal process that runs through several systems that do not talk to each other in plain language:

- A **court** changes your legal name, and sometimes your legal sex.
- **Social Security** has to know your new name before many DMVs will issue a card.
- The **DMV** changes your license.
- The **vital records office in the state where you were born** changes your birth certificate, under its own rules.
- **Federal agencies** changed their own rules in 2025.

Each office explains only its own step. Court self-help pages are written for people who already know what a petition and a judgment are. Legal aid groups do important work and cannot reach everyone who needs them. The result shows up in the data: in the 2022 U.S. Trans Survey, 48% of respondents with at least one ID said none of their IDs had the name they wanted ([NCTE, February 2024](https://ustranssurvey.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/2022-USTS-Early-Insights-Report_FINAL.pdf)).

## What The Strand Project does

1. **Shows the whole path, in order.** Court, then Social Security, then the license, then the birth certificate, then everything else.
2. **Personalizes it.** Four questions (where you live, what you are changing, who it is for, where you were born) produce only the steps that apply.
3. **Proves every fact.** Every fact links to the official source and shows the date we checked it.
4. **Says what it does not know.** "Not confirmed" and "Our reading" labels mark every gap.
5. **Protects the reader.** No tracking, a Leave quickly button, and nothing saved without consent.
6. **Works for every brain.** Smart Brevity structure, short sentences, checklists, a short version, and reading settings.

## Who it is for

Trans, nonbinary, and intersex people changing their legal name or gender marker in Oregon, Washington, or Utah, and the parents, partners, friends, case workers, and advocates helping them.

## Principles

- **Official sources first, opened and read.** Search results are leads, never support.
- **Plain words.** Reading level at or below grade 8, measured on every build.
- **Dated and current.** A source older than 120 days triggers a re-check.
- **No legal advice.** We explain the process. We are not anyone's lawyer.
- **Independent.** Not run by, or connected to, any court, agency, or employer.
- **Open.** Every decision and change is in the public history.

## What success looks like

- A person can go from "where do I start" to the right official form in under five minutes.
- Every fact on the site has a source and a date, and none is older than 120 days.
- People who are disabled or neurodivergent can complete the path without help from the site's makers.
- Legal aid groups and court self-help staff link to it because it saves them time.

## Out of scope, for now

- States other than Oregon, Washington, and Utah. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
- Medical care, insurance, and employment law.
- Filling out or filing forms for people.
