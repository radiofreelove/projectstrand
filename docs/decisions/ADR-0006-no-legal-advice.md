# ADR-0006: Explain the process, never advise

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-03

## Context

Readers need clear direction, but the project is not a law firm and cannot know anyone's full situation.

## Decision

- The site describes what the law, forms, and agencies say, and what the steps are. It does not tell anyone which option to choose.
- Where the law is silent or unclear, the site says so, labels any interpretation "Our reading," and points to a lawyer or legal aid.
- Situations that raise legal risk (a pending lawsuit, criminal history, immigration status, a minor without parental support) get an explicit "talk to a lawyer" pointer.
- Every page footer says: "We explain the process. We are not your lawyer."
- Issues and corrections must not include personal case details.

## Consequences

The guide stays accurate and safe to share by courts, legal aid, and advocates. Some readers will still need a lawyer, and the help page makes that easy.
