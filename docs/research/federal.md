# Research memo: federal records

Checked: November 3, 2026. Labels: (unverified) = could not confirm in an official source; (inference) = our reasoning beyond the sources.

## Issue

What can a person expect when updating name and sex on a U.S. passport and a Social Security record, as of November 3, 2026?

## Short answer

Passports: under Executive Order 14168 and the State Department policy that followed, new and renewed passports show the sex assigned at birth, and the X marker is no longer offered. Advocacy groups warn that any new application, including a name change, can result in a passport with the sex assigned at birth. The policy is in force while litigation continues. Social Security: name changes after a legal name change are still processed; sex marker changes are not.

## Authority

- **Executive Order 14168**, January 20, 2025, section 3(d): the Secretaries of State and Homeland Security and the OPM Director "shall implement ... government-issued identification documents, including passports, visas, and Global Entry cards," that "accurately reflect the holder's sex" as the order defines it. [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/defending-women-from-gender-ideology-extremism-and-restoring-biological-truth-to-the-federal-government/)
- **Human Rights Campaign, Travel and Passport Rights for Transgender People** (reflects April 2026; secondary): new and renewed passports show the sex assigned at birth "regardless of gender identity, legal name change, or any other documentation"; the X marker "available since 2022 ... was eliminated"; "The Social Security Administration also no longer permits sex marker changes, and the State Department may cross-reference SSA records"; a name change "could trigger the State Department to update the sex marker on your passport to match your birth sex"; if asked for early public records, do not respond "before consulting an LGBTQ+-affirming attorney or legal organization." [HRC](https://www.hrc.org/resources/travel-passport-rights-for-transgender-people)
- **ACLU, Orr v. Trump** (last update October 2, 2026; secondary): "anyone who applies for a new, corrected, or replacement passport, or for a passport renewal is at risk of having their passport issued bearing the sex they were assigned at birth"; as of September 2026 the case is in discovery in the trial court, continuing "at least until Spring 2027." [ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/cases/orr-v-trump)
- **Social Security, Change your name**: update "after you legally change it" by requesting a replacement card; "you may be able to request your change online." [SSA](https://www.ssa.gov/personal-record/change-name)
- **Oregon DMV** (official, state): "As of Jan. 31, 2025, the Trump administration has directed the Social Security Administration to stop processing gender marker updates." [Oregon DMV](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/DMV/Pages/driverid/chg_gender_designation.aspx)

## Application

- Readers with a passport that already matches them should weigh whether to apply for a new one now; the site says to talk to a lawyer if unsure (inference).
- Name changes at SSA should come before DMV visits in states whose DMVs verify names with SSA (Oregon confirms it does).

## Contrary authority and uncertainty

1. **State Department text.** The State Department's sex marker pages blocked our tools. Search results said the department issues only M or F matching sex at birth and that passports issued before the change stay valid until they expire (unverified). The site labels this "Not confirmed."
2. **Procedural history.** HRC reports a preliminary injunction in June 2025 and a Supreme Court order in November 2025 allowing enforcement while the case continues. A search result reported that on June 5, 2026, the First Circuit dismissed the appeal as moot and vacated the injunction (unverified; not used on the site). We did not open court dockets in this pass. Per our method, we report status from the most recent source we opened and do not characterize any ruling as settled law.
3. **SSA sex marker page.** The former SSA page on changing sex identification now redirects to the SSA home page (checked November 3, 2026).
