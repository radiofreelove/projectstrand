# Research memo: Oregon

Checked: November 3, 2026. Labels: (unverified) = could not confirm in an official source; (inference) = our reasoning beyond the sources.

## Issue

How does an adult, or a parent for a minor, change legal name and legal sex in Oregon courts, and update an Oregon birth record and Oregon DMV record, as of November 3, 2026?

## Short answer

One petition can ask for a name change, a legal change of sex, or both. A name-only petition is filed in the circuit court of the county where the person lives. A sex change, alone or with a name change, can be filed in any circuit court. The court may grant a legal change of sex if the person attests to "surgical, hormonal or other treatment appropriate for the individual for the purpose of affirming gender identity." Since July 1, 2026, cases that include a legal change of sex are confidential by statute; name-only cases remain public unless the applicant is a certified Address Confidentiality Program participant who asks for sealing. The filing fee is $124. Oregon birth records and Oregon DMV records can be changed without a court order.

## Authority

- **ORS 33.410** (2025 edition): name change heard in the county where the person resides; "The change of name shall be granted by the court unless the court finds that the change is not consistent with the public interest." [ORS chapter 33](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors033.html)
- **ORS 33.420**: written notice to both parents and any guardian before a minor's name change, with an exception when the child has not lived with a parent and that parent has not contributed or tried to contribute support (subsections 1 and 2). Sealing on request for certified ACP participants in name change cases (subsection 3). Subsection 4 (seal on request in sex change cases) was deleted by 2026 Or Laws ch 52, section 11.
- **ORS 33.440**: when a minor applies for a name change, the court may confer with the child and exclude the parents.
- **ORS 33.460(1) to (3)**: any circuit court may hear a legal change of sex and a simultaneous name and sex change; attestation standard quoted above.
- **2026 Oregon Laws chapter 52 (HB 4088), section 12**, adding ORS 33.460(4): a case for legal change of sex, including a simultaneous name and sex change, "is confidential, exempt from public disclosure" and may be disclosed only to a party or the party's attorney, the court, by court order for good cause (subject to ORS 192.324), or as required for notice under ORS 33.420. The applicant may move to make the case public. On the applicant's motion, the court shall make the case confidential if it is not already. Section 17: applies to applications filed on or after July 1, 2026, and, on motion, to earlier sex change applications. Section 18: operative July 1, 2026. Section 20: emergency clause. [Session law PDF](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/lawsstatutes/2026orLaw0052.pdf)
- **OJD Name and Sex Change Packet (Adult), forms dated July 2026**: filing locations; "All records of legal sex change petitions, including changes to both sex and name, are confidential"; no court appearance unless the court says so; judgment effective when entered; Notice of Entry of Judgment; the court does not send the judgment to other agencies; a lawyer is not required; petition options male, female, nonbinary; attestation checkbox; public interest disclosures; ACP sealing box; declaration under penalty of perjury. [Packet PDF](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/forms/Documents/Name%20and%20Sex%20Change%20Packet%20(Adult).pdf)
- **OJD Circuit Court Fee Schedule, effective January 1, 2026**: change of name or change of sex $124 (ORS 21.145); certified copy $5 plus 25 cents per page for paper; fee waiver or deferral application, no fee (ORS 21.682). [Fee schedule PDF](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/Documents/2026_CircuitFeeSchedule_public_eff-2026-01-01.pdf)
- **OHA, Change Birth Record to Support Gender Identity**: notarized application (OHA 2673), no court order; name and sex can each be changed once this way; M, F, or X; $35 amendment fee; sex change leaves no footnote and removes existing footnotes; name-only administrative change leaves a footnote without the prior name; OHA honors court orders from Oregon and other states; other agencies may require a court order. [OHA page](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/PH/BIRTHDEATHCERTIFICATES/Pages/ChangeBirthSupportGenderIdentity.aspx)
- **OHA, court order route**: court-ordered name change footnote references the prior name; a sealed order (or one requiring a new certificate) allows a new record without that footnote. [OHA page](https://oregon.gov/oha/PH/BIRTHDEATHCERTIFICATES/CHANGEVITALRECORDS/Pages/CourtOrderChangeSex.aspx)
- **Oregon DMV, Changing Your Gender Marker**: choose M, F, or X ("not specified"); standard and REAL ID; in person; pay renewal or replacement fee; DMV keeps a history of issuances and names; data shared with the National Driver Register, Selective Service, and LEDS; change name with SSA first; states SSA was directed to stop processing gender marker updates as of January 31, 2025. [DMV page](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/DMV/Pages/driverid/chg_gender_designation.aspx)

## Application

- Adults: Guide & File or the PDF packet; check name, sex, or both; file in the right court; pay $124 or apply for a waiver or deferral; wait for the judgment; order certified copies; update records in order (SSA for name, then DMV, then birth record).
- Privacy: a person who wants a name change kept out of public records can file it together with a sex change, which makes the case confidential. A name-only case is public unless the ACP rule applies. This is a description of the statute, not a recommendation.
- Minors: a parent, legal guardian, or legal representative files the minor packet (adult packet instructions). Notice rules in ORS 33.420 apply to name changes.

## Contrary authority and uncertainty

1. **Footnotes after confidential cases.** OHA's footnote exception is written for "sealed" name change orders. Whether OHA treats a 2026 confidential sex change case the same way is not addressed on the pages we read (unverified). The site tells readers to ask OHA first.
2. **Out-of-state residents.** ORS 33.460 says "any circuit court in this state" and does not mention residence. We found no official statement on non-residents (inference: possible, but untested here). The site labels this "Our reading" and says to talk to a lawyer.
3. **Minor-initiated petitions.** ORS 33.440 contemplates a minor applying for a name change, but the OJD packet says a parent, guardian, or legal representative files for a minor. The site points minors to a lawyer.
4. **Fee for a combined petition.** The fee schedule lists change of name and change of sex at $124. We read it as one fee for one combined petition (inference).
5. **DMV fee amount.** The DMV page links to a fee table we did not open. The site says "renewal or replacement fee" without a number (unverified amount).
6. **2025 HB 2309.** Not reflected in the 2025 ORS edition (inference: not enacted).
7. **One-parent signature for minors at OHA.** An earlier note cited ORS 432.235(3)(a); we did not re-open it in this pass, so the site says only that a parent or legal guardian applies.
