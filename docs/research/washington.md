# Research memo: Washington

Checked: November 3, 2026. Labels: (unverified) = could not confirm in an official source; (inference) = our reasoning beyond the sources.

## Issue

How does a person in Washington change their legal name in court, including through a sealed proceeding, and change the sex designation on a Washington birth certificate and a Washington driver license or ID, as of November 3, 2026?

## Short answer

A name change can be filed in any district court in Washington as a public case, or in any superior court as a sealed case when the reason relates to gender expression or identity (among other listed reasons). In a sealed case there is no public access to the filing, proceeding, or order. People under Department of Corrections jurisdiction or required to register as sex offenders cannot use the sealed route. Washington's birth certificate and license sex designations change by form, with M, F, or X, and no court order. The Department of Health does not accept a court order or a doctor's letter for a sex designation change.

## Authority

- **RCW 4.24.130** (official PDF certified July 12, 2024; last amended 2023 c 34): (1) petition in "the district court of any judicial district in the state"; (2) and (3) rules for people under DOC jurisdiction and registered sex offenders; (4) "the court shall waive all fees for filing and recording a name change order" on an affidavit of financial hardship by the person or a qualified legal service provider; (5)(a) name change petitions "may be filed and shall be heard in any superior court" when the reason is "related to gender expression or identity as defined in RCW 49.60.040," among other reasons; (5)(c) the superior court shall seal the file, and "there shall be no public access to any court record of the name change filing, proceeding, or order," with exceptions for a court order on good cause or the person's own request; (5)(d) subsection 5 does not apply to people under subsections 2 or 3. [RCW 4.24.130 PDF](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/RCWPDF/RCW%20%20%204%20%20TITLE/RCW%20%20%204%20.%2024%20%20CHAPTER/RCW%20%20%204%20.%2024%20.130.pdf)
- **2025 RCW Sections Affected**: no entry for RCW 4.24.130. [2025 table](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/CRO/SectionsAffected/2025%20RCW%20SL%20Sections%20Affected%20in%20Bill%20Order.pdf)
- **DOH, Sex designation change on a birth certificate**: M, F, X; forms DOH 422-143 (adults and emancipated minors, notarized) and DOH 422-144 (minors); "Submitting the form is required as of January 27, 2018. A court order or a letter from your physician will not be accepted."; complies with WAC 246-490-075; name differences require a certified court order with DOH 422-126; about 3 business days to process plus up to 2 weeks for mail; by mail or in person by appointment; no fee to amend, $25 per certified copy. [DOH page](https://doh.wa.gov/licenses-permits-and-certificates/vital-records/sex-designation-change-birth-certificate)
- **DOL, Change your gender designation**: M, F, X ("not exclusively male or female"); in person by appointment (verbal confirmation) or by mail with form 520043; no online option; free if only the gender designation changes. [DOL page](https://dol.wa.gov/driver-licenses-and-permits/update-driver-license-information/change-your-gender-designation)
- **WashingtonLawHelp (Northwest Justice Project), Name Change Guide, reviewed May 8, 2025** (secondary): forms and instructions; no newspaper publication; a hearing for every name change; sealed superior court fee usually about $260; certified copies about $5, get 7 or more; special DOL process for sealed orders; notice for minors and child consent at 13 or older in some courts. [Guide](https://www.washingtonlawhelp.org/en/name-change-guide)
- **WashingtonLawHelp, court order that confirms your gender, reviewed July 18, 2025** (secondary): optional; not needed for a Washington license or birth certificate. [Guide](https://www.washingtonlawhelp.org/en/get-court-order-confirms-your-gender-change)
- **Washington Courts forms pages**: CLEAR referral line (888) 201-1014; courthouse facilitators "cannot provide legal advice"; GR 34 motion to waive civil fees. [Forms](https://www.courts.wa.gov/forms/)

## Application

- Gender-related name change: superior court, sealed. Any other name change: district court, public.
- Fee relief: the district court must waive filing and recording fees on a hardship affidavit; GR 34 applies in any court.
- Birth certificate and license: forms only. A court order confirming gender is optional and may help with other states' vital records offices.
